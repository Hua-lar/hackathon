释放生活价值,重塑 Web3 收益模式 | Unleash Life Value, Reshaping Web3 Earning
项目简介 | Introduction
Life2Earn 是一款专为“非技术背景人群”打造的 Web3 职业代理平台。我们认为,每个人的生活
经历都拥有独特的链上价值。
Life2Earn is a Web3 career agency platform designed for "non-technical individuals." We believe
everyone's life experiences possess unique on-chain value.
项目核心是通过 AI Agent 捕捉用户的“生活价值”并自动匹配任务,结合 Solana 智能合约实现自
动化的资金托管与结算。
The project uses AI Agents to capture "life value" and match tasks, with Solana smart contracts for
automated escrow and settlement.
核心功能 | Features
1. AI 价值解析引擎 | AI Value Parser
用户通过自然语言与 AI 聊天,AI 自动提取性格标签、软技能及潜在岗位,生成“灵魂简历”。
Users chat with AI in natural language; AI extracts personality tags and skills to generate a "Soul
Resume."
2. 智能资金托管 | Smart Escrow
企业预存报酬至合约(Lock),保障用户利益;一旦确认验收,资金立即自动划转。
Employers pre-deposit rewards into the contract; funds transfer automatically upon task acceptance.
3. 无感结算体验 | Seamless Settlement
针对小白用户设计的“Gas 费抵扣”逻辑,直接从工资中扣除微量手续费,用户无需提前持有
SOL。
Gas Fee Deduction logic: fees are deducted directly from the payout, no initial SOL required.
